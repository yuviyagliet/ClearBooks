Implementation Notes

Typography: “Clear” Light 300 + “Books” SemiBold 600, tracking -1.4, Inter / SF Pro Display. Tagline 9.5pt, tracking 4.2, Slate-400. Perfect for creative studios — soft luxury, not corporate.

How it reads: At a glance it’s a geometric “C”. On second look the two diamond facets on the right form “B” — the spine is shared. The counter cut-outs ensure instant legibility at 20px.

No icon clichés: Zero books, coins, calculators. The prism/diamond chamfers themselves are the metaphor — clarity & precision.

Files: All SVGs use pure vectors, shape-rendering: geometricPrecision, integer coordinates, miter joins. Replace counter fill (#0F172A ↔ #F8FAFC) to swap dark/light.
